description: "Assess a product or environment against selected compliance frameworks and report evidence coverage."
---

Run a compliance assessment using the compliance-check skill. Load .claude/skills/compliance-check/SKILL.md and follow its full procedure. Resolve the declared boundary with `traust compliance resolve <boundary>`; environment collectors also require the authorized targets.yaml. Check framework/ADR staleness, collect snapshots (skip-and-record), and run `traust compliance evaluate <boundary> --frameworks <selection> --out <out>/compliance-assessment.json` with the user's declared inputs and collector paths (`--crypto <snapshot>` for crypto facts). Read the returned JSON/Markdown paths and coverage, judge only evidence_review controls twice (disagreement stays not_assessed), and re-run `traust compliance check` after edits. For failing crypto controls, hand the gated JSON, Markdown companion, cited crypto snapshot and checkout/ref to /crypto-analysis for evidence-backed remediation ownership, not a replacement verdict. Record the gated assessment with `traust compliance record` before rebuilding `traust dashboard view compliance`. Report coverage per framework without a blended percentage; keep skipped frameworks, caveats and unknowns explicit. PCI CDE boundary, GDPR personal-data stores and SOC 2 trust categories come from the user, never inference.
